---
title: "Sonnet 5"
created: 2026-04-16
---

At times I grasp my pen to summon Muses—
instead I’m met by gasping, drowning Choice,
which struggling to stay floating, self-abuses.
So ends my song before I’ve found my voice.

Now voiceless I am subject to your leering;
your judgment is foretold, a muffled scream
that fills my future — all-conjoining fearing,
a stifling stillness, this ensqualored dream.

For what I know, and what I can accept,
stay silent in me ‘til I hear you speak
the obvious. Oh, were I more adept,
I’d spend less effort keeping myself weak.

When I release this raft to which I cling,
I listen to your love, and start to sing.
