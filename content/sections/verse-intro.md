Here are a few of the poems I've written. 

Poetry for me is music foremost. Or a tree! A half-silly, half-sacred syllable game.  

Of course, I think I should use my words as more than tasteful honking. I do try, and maybe you will find some meaning here or there. Mind you though, this isn’t the page where I try to be strictly coherent, nor exactly represent myself.

Now hear! As I bid the oft-neglected deity, Debris: *suffuse this fluff with the all-blessing glow of your trash fire!*
